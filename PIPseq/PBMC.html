2Protocol

I used 10 × 10⁶ PBMC for PIPseq sample prep. Tick steps as you go — ticks persist in this browser.

  1. Warm complete medium to 37 °C: RPMI-1640 + 10% FBS or human serum.
  2. Remove PBMC vial from liquid nitrogen or −80 °C.
  3. Thaw quickly in a 37 °C water bath until only a tiny ice crystal remains, usually 1–2 min.
  4. Immediately move to the hood. Transfer cells gently into a 15 mL tube.
  5. Add warm complete medium slowly, dropwise at first, while gently swirling.
    • 1 mL thawed PBMC + 9 mL warm complete medium
  6. Spin: 400 g, 8 min, RT or 4 °C.
  7. Aspirate supernatant carefully to remove DMSO. Resuspend gently in 1 mL complete medium, then add 9 mL complete medium.
  8. Rest 10 mL PBMC (2 M/mL): 1 h at 37 °C, 5% CO₂. Then wash into cold binding buffer before panning.
    • (option) Add DNase I 25 µg/mL
    • Overnight culture at 1–2 M/mL, 37 °C is fine if cells must carry to the next day
    • Double-frozen PBMC lose 30–50% viability and dead cells drive nonspecific capture — freeze protocol in §4
  9. Count viable cells by diluting ×5. Check viability with trypan blue.
    • Trypan blue: 10 µL cell suspension + 10 µL 0.4% trypan blue
    • Usually above 90%. Record viability each time. Illumina wants >80%
    • Strain 40 µm before counting if any clumps are visible
  10. Take 10 × 10⁶ PBMC, wash twice, resuspend at approximately 10 × 10⁶ PBMC/mL.
    • Washing twice is crucial to the Sulfo-NHS-biotin reaction — serum amines quench the ester on contact
    • Wash into PBS pH 8.0, not binding buffer (no BSA at this step)
  11. Dissolve 1 mg Sulfo-NHS-biotin in 1 mL PBS pH 8.0. Mix 1 mL reagent + 1 mL of the 10 M PBMC suspension.
    • Arithmetic: 1 mg / 2 mL = 0.5 mg/mL final, matching ~5 mL of 0.5 mg/mL per 2.5 × 10⁷ cells. One tube = one 10 M reaction.
    • Cell density during the reaction is 5 × 10⁶/mL, not 10⁷ — the equal volume of reagent halves it. That is the vendor condition. For 10⁷/mL during labeling, resuspend at 2 × 10⁷/mL in Step 10
  12. Incubate 30 min, 4 °C, gentle rotation. Quench 5 min at 4 °C, then wash 3–4 times with cold binding buffer.
    • 3 washes minimum — residual free biotin binds streptavidin tighter than anything else and consumes bead capacity before the beads reach a cell
    • Recount here and compare to Step 9
  13. Add pre-cleared yeast library to biotinylated target PBMC. Incubate 1 h, 4 °C, gentle rotation.
    • (option) Fc block 10–15 min at 4 °C before adding library
    • Record the yeast:PBMC ratio
  14. Add Dynabeads MyOne Streptavidin T1 (#65601) to the PBMC–library mixture.
    • 10/5/26: tried 10 µL for 1 million — too much beads
    • 10 mg/mL ≈ 7–12 × 10⁹ beads/mL, so 10 µL = 70–120 beads per PBMC. Target 5–10 beads/cell → ~1 µL per 10⁶ cells
    • For 10 × 10⁶ cells: ~10 µL. Below that, dilute stock 1:10 and take 10 µL of the dilution
  15. Incubate 15 min, 4 °C.
  16. Apply to magnetic capture. Wait 5 min, remove supernatant, add CSB with the tube on the stand to wash.
    • Keep the supernatant as the unbound fraction until capture is confirmed
  17. Add 1 mL CSB, count with hemocytometer. Prepare 5,700 complexes/µL (or 5.7 million/mL) and add 3 µL to PIP.
    • Count complexes (although claude suggest count bodies)
    • Strain 40 µm before diluting and then count

5Appendix — freezing leftover PBMC

If you can not use PBMC immediately, overnight culture at 1–2 × 10⁶/mL, 37 °C, 5% CO₂ in a vented flask if you need cells within 24 h. Re-freezing costs another 30–50% viability, biased against monocytes and NK cells.

Only freeze from cells ≥85% viable.

  1. Wash once in complete medium; count and record viability.
  2. Spin 300–400 g, 8 min. Remove supernatant completely — residual medium dilutes the cryoprotectant.
  3. Resuspend in cold FBS at 2× final density.
  4. Prepare 20% DMSO in FBS, ice cold.
  5. Add the DMSO/FBS dropwise to an equal volume of cells over ~1 min, swirling. Bolus addition is the main cause of poor recovery. Final: 90% FBS / 10% DMSO.
  6. 2–5 × 10⁶ per vial — sparser than a first freeze; sparse vials thaw better.
  7. Aliquot on ice. Total DMSO exposure before freezing <10–15 min.
  8. −1 °C/min (CoolCell / Mr. Frosty) at −80 °C for 12–24 h.
  9. Move to LN₂ vapor within 24–72 h. PBMC degrade at −80 °C over weeks.
  10. Label vials double-frozen so a later run doesn’t mix them with first-thaw cells.
CryoStor CS10 replaces steps 3–5 and removes the dropwise step.
